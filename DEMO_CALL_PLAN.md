# Two-call demonstration plan

## Solo: split shipment — BB1046

You are the customer. Record your microphone and browser audio. Aim for 60–75 seconds of useful interaction.

1. Say: “My order is B B one zero four six. My email is taylor at example dot com. Where are my items?”
2. After Robin explains the packages: “Do you have a confirmed arrival date for the cap?”
3. When answered accurately: “That answers my question, thank you. That's all.”

This shows verification, live TrackingMore lookup, per-package handling, a tracked T-shirt versus an unshipped cap, and no invented arrival date. Accept the actual carrier event wording; do not force an out-for-delivery label. End the call and check its actual dashboard outcome before describing it as resolved.

## Helper customer: delivered but missing — BB1043

You are the rep on /support; the helper is the customer on /voice. Use separate devices and headphones. Mark yourself available before the call. Aim for 75–100 seconds of useful interaction.

Helper says:

1. “My order is B B one zero four three. My email is jamie at example dot com. Where is my package?”
2. After Robin states delivered and asks whether received: “No, I haven't received it. I checked with my household and reception, and it's not there. Please connect me to a person.”
3. After you join: “That's right. I checked those places but couldn't find it.”

Review the brief BEFORE joining, select Join customer, and say:

“Hi, I'm the Bird Box support rep. I can see your package was marked delivered, but you haven't received it, and you've already checked with your household and reception. Is that correct?”

Exchange a fresh sentence each to show both humans can hear one another. You can say “I can help you review the next steps from here.” Do not claim a refund, investigation, ticket, or address change that the demo does not perform. The helper ends the customer call.

This shows carrier-marked delivered does not prove receipt, appropriate escalation, an issue brief, a real person joining, Robin silent, and saved history.

## Important: record the complete handoff on the customer device

Before you join, your support browser is NOT in the audio room. OBS on the rep computer cannot capture Robin's earlier conversation simply through desktop audio.

Record the helper's CUSTOMER device with its microphone plus browser audio to capture the helper, Robin, and your human voice after joining. Optionally record your SUPPORT screen too, then insert the brief/Join customer visuals while retaining the customer recording as the continuous audio source. Avoid mixing two copies of the same conversation audio.

Alternatively, let the helper use your OBS-equipped computer as the customer, while you use a second device for the support desk. Keep recording on the customer device.

Play back a short test first. Vapi's own observed recording omits the human rep, so do not rely on it for complete handoff audio.
